- الفكرة: A gas station on a desolate highway that gradually erases the memories and identities of its patrons, acting as a threshold or collector of forgotten selves for those seeking escape from their painful pasts.
- الصراع: An individual's desperate struggle to retain their identity and memories against the station's insidious influence, eventually confronting the choice between embracing oblivion or facing a painful past.
- الشخصيات: Jamal (weary traveler, subconsciously seeking escape/oblivion from a tragic past), The Attendant (silent, unnerving, ancient guardian/custodian of the threshold), Khalil (truck driver, already lost to the station's influence, serving as a warning).
- المكان: A desolate, dimly lit gas station on an empty highway at night, seemingly out of place and time, with an eerie, silent atmosphere.
- الافتتاحية: A weary driver, Jamal, stumbles upon a gas station on a dark, desolate highway, drawn by an almost hypnotic, faint light and an overwhelming need for fuel.
- المفاجأة: The station doesn't actively steal memories; it collects identities willingly (or subconsciously) shed by those seeking an escape from their past burdens and pain. Jamal himself was unconsciously seeking this oblivion.
- النهاية: Jamal accepts his fate, his identity fading as he becomes another silent, placid presence at the station, joining its timeless cycle of forgotten souls, having found peace in total oblivion.
